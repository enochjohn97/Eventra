Cookie Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Effective: 21 October 2026

This policy explains how EventOut Inc. ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies such as local storage and device identifiers on the Service, which includes the website at EventOut-website.liveblog365.com and the EventOut mobile app.

By using the Service, you agree to the use of cookies as described here. You can control or disable cookies through your browser, device, or app settings though doing so may limit features like sign-in, ticket storage, and personalisation.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small pieces of data stored on your device by your browser or app. They help us recognise you, remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how the Service is used. "Similar technologies" include local storage, session storage, and device or advertising identifiers that perform the same basic function as cookies.

3. Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Service to work. They support sign-in, security, ticket verification, and basic browsing. You cannot opt out of essential cookies while using the Service disabling them in your browser will break features like staying signed in and viewing your tickets.

4. Performance & analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how the Service is used so we can improve it. They do not identify you personally where we can avoid it; we rely on aggregated or anonymised data where possible. You can usually opt out of analytics cookies through your browser settings, though doing so may limit our ability to improve the Service.

5. Functional & preference cookies

These cookies remember your choices and settings to make the Service more convenient for example, your preferred sort order or the filters you last applied. They are not essential, but the Service is less personalised without them.

6. Cookies in the mobile app

The EventOut mobile app uses local storage and device identifiers in addition to cookies where the app's web views use them. These serve the same purposes described above authentication, ticketing, preferences, and analytics. The app does not use third-party advertising identifiers for advertising based on your activity across other apps or websites.

7. How to control cookies

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Browser settings: most browsers let you block, delete, or warn you about cookies. You can usually find these settings in your browser's privacy or security menu.
  • Device settings: on mobile devices, you can manage location, advertising, and storage permissions in your device settings.
  • App settings: you can manage the app's access to storage, location, and other features through your device's app settings.

If you disable cookies or similar technologies, some features of the Service may stop working in particular, sign-in, ticket storage, and personalised recommendations.
